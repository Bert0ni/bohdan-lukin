Scope Specification Document (SSD) — TEMPLATE

Basis: ECB TIBER-EU Scope Specification Document Guidance, January 2025 (../assets/source/ecb-ssd-guidance-2025.pdf), §2 "Required content" + Annexes 1–3, cross-checked against the binding DORA TLPT RTS, Annex II (Reg. (EU) 2025/1190, ../assets/source/Delegated-Regulation-EU-2025-1190-DORA-TLPT-RTS.md). ⚠️ The RTS adds authenticity to the flag security aspects (C/I/Au/A), where the ECB guidance listed only C/I/A. Owner: CT/CTL (entity). Validated by: the authority / TM (reviews scenario selection to ensure the test focuses on systemically critical CIFs). We (RTTM) may consult on technical feasibility only. Not just a list of IPs. Copy per engagement; do not edit this blank.

Part A — ECB §2 compliance spine (the SSD shall contain, at a minimum)#

  • A1 — A list of ALL CIFs identified by the financial entity (not only those in scope).
  • A2 — Per CIF, an inclusion/exclusion rationale: where in scope, the reasons for its inclusion; where not in scope, the reasons for its exclusion.
  • A3 — The identified ICT system(s) supporting each CIF.
  • A4 — Per ICT system: whether it is outsourced and, if so, the name of the ICT third-party service provider; and the jurisdiction(s) in which the system is used.
  • A5 — A high-level description of preliminary flag(s), indicating which security aspect — confidentiality, integrity, authenticity or availability — each flag covers (RTS Annex II; the ECB guidance omitted authenticity — follow the RTS).

Annexes in the ECB guidance to reuse: Annex 1 executive-summary template · Annex 2 CIF / subfunction / justification template · Annex 3 flag-setting template.

Part B — Drafting layout#

1. Document control#

Version · classification · author (CTL) · approvals (entity leadership) · authority/TM validation · distribution.

2. Critical or Important Functions (CIFs)#

List every CIF the entity has identified; for each, state whether it is in scope and the explicit reason for inclusion, or out of scope and the explicit reason for exclusion (A1, A2).

3. CIF → supporting ICT systems → flags (A3, A4, A5)#

CIF (in/out of scope + reason)Supporting ICT system(s)Outsourced? → ICT third-party providerJurisdiction(s) of usePreliminary flag + security aspect (C / I / Authenticity / A)
(e.g. Payment processing — IN: systemic)Payment gateway, IAM, processing backendYes → provider nameDE, SKControlled proof of ability to influence the payment process — Integrity
(e.g. Customer banking — IN)Web/API, authentication, backendNoSKProof of unauthorised access to a defined test resource — Confidentiality
(e.g. Treasury operations — IN)Trading infrastructure, privileged accessYes → provider nameSK, LUProof of reaching an agreed privileged position / disrupting a process — Availability

4. In-scope environment#

Production systems/services, external attack surface (IPs, domains, apps, APIs, cloud endpoints), people/locations in scope.

5. Exclusions & boundaries#

Out-of-scope systems, prohibited actions, third-party systems not to be actively tested.

6. Risk controls & constraints#

Testing windows, change freezes, data-handling rules, legal/regulatory constraints.

7. Flags — detailed definitions#

For each flag: ID, description, CIF, acceptance criteria (what proves it), evidence required.

8. Approvals#

Entity leadership sign-off · authority validation · date/version.
