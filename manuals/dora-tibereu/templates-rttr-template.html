Red Team Test Report (RTTR) — TEMPLATE

Basis: ECB TIBER-EU Red Team Test Report Guidance, January 2025 (../assets/source/ecb-rttr-guidance-2025.pdf), §2 "Required content" + §3 considerations, mirroring the binding DORA TLPT RTS, Annex V (Reg. (EU) 2025/1190, ../assets/source/Delegated-Regulation-EU-2025-1190-DORA-TLPT-RTS.md). Owner: RT / RTTM. Due to the CT within 4 weeks of the end of active testing (TIBER-EU 2025). Feeds TSR and Remediation Plan. Copy per engagement; do not edit this blank.

Part A — ECB §2 compliance spine (the RTTR shall include at least ALL of these)#

  • A1 — Information on the performed attack, including: the targeted CIFs and the ICT systems/processes/technologies supporting them (as identified in the RTTP) · a summary of each scenario · flags reached and not reached · attack paths followed successfully and unsuccessfully · TTPs used successfully and unsuccessfully · deviations from the RTTP (if any) · leg-ups granted (if any).
  • A2 — All actions the testers are aware of that were performed by the BT to reconstruct the attack and mitigate its effects.
  • A3 — Discovered vulnerabilities and other findings, including: description and criticality · root-cause analysis of successful attacks · recommendations for remediation, each with a remediation-priority indication.

§3 considerations: write a management summary in layman's terms for senior management/Board; a per-scenario storyline (end-to-end, in/through/out where relevant, MITRE ATT&CK-mapped, flags/objectives vs the CIA triad, deviations, leg-ups); then findings, provisional root cause analysis, recommendations, artefacts, and a confidentiality handling note.

Part B — Drafting layout#

1. Document control#

Version · classification · author (RTTM) · reviewers · distribution · change history.

2. Executive summary#

Outcome vs objectives/flags · key risks · headline detection/response observations · priority recommendations.

3. Scope & methodology#

CIFs, flags, scenarios, approach; reference to SSD, TTIR, RTTP.

4. Executed scenarios#

Per scenario: narrative, attack path taken, result (flag achieved / not), timeline.

5. TTPs used#

Mapped to MITRE ATT&CK; successful vs unsuccessful; what was detected.

6. Flags#

Status of each flag (achieved / partial / not) with evidence references.

7. Deviations & leg-ups#

All approved deviations and leg-ups: what, why, when, who approved.

8. Evidence#

Key evidence with timestamps and references to the evidence register.

9. Vulnerabilities & root causes#

Findings, severity, root-cause analysis (not just symptoms). Draft one card per finding with the finding template and roll them up into the finding register here.

10. Detection & response observations#

What the defence saw/missed (input to Replay/Purple and BTTR comparison).

11. Recommendations#

Root-cause-oriented, prioritised; input to the entity's Remediation Plan; resilience roadmap.

12. Appendices#

Detailed evidence, IOC/TTP tables, tool/infrastructure notes, cleanup confirmation.
