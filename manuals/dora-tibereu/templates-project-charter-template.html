Project Charter — TEMPLATE

Basis: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1190 (DORA TLPT RTS), Annex I — "Content of the project charter (Article 9(2)(a))" (full text). New under the RTS — this is a binding preparation-phase deliverable that did not exist as a named artifact in the pre-DORA TIBER-EU framework. Owner: Control Team Lead (CTL) / entity. We (TIP + RT) are named in it and consulted, but do not own it. Produced in the preparation phase (Art. 9). Copy per engagement; do not edit this blank.

Supporting sections (recommended)#

  • Document control — version, classification, author (CTL), TM awareness, distribution, change history.
  • Governance & stakeholders — CT members, TM / TLPT authority, TIP, RT (RTTM), and the single-provider separation arrangement (cross-reference the TI-to-RT handover register).
  • Risk management summary — link to the RoE and safety-and-risk.
  • Links — this charter precedes and frames the SSD; the SSD's scope must reconcile with the CIFs listed here.
