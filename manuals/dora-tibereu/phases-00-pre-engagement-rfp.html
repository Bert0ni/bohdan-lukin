Phase 0 — Pre-engagement / RFP / Procurement

Timing: before or M0–M6 · Owner: Authority/TM + CT (we respond as provider)

Where our work actually begins as a provider — winning and contracting the engagement.

Activities#

  • Regulatory notification; authority appoints TM; entity forms CT/CTL + initiation docs.
  • Entity issues RFP/RFI for TIP and RT (joint or split procurement).
  • We respond with technical + commercial proposals.
  • Due diligence on qualifications and independence; vendor selection; contracting.
  • BT is not informed.

Our RFP response package#

DocumentContents
Technical proposalMethodology, execution model, governance
Team compositionRTTM, operators, specialists, backup resources
Experience & referencesRelevant red-team engagements
Methodology statementThreat-led adversary simulation, MITRE ATT&CK, safety
Risk management approachProduction safety, escalation, stop conditions
Delivery planPhases, milestones, dependencies
Commercial proposalPrice, resources, scope-change terms

Gate / exit criteria#

  • Procurement completed, contracts (MSA/SOW/NDA/data-protection/insurance) signed, providers onboarded — before testing starts.

Inputs → Outputs#

  • In: RFP/RFI, preliminary scope, objectives.
  • Out: signed contracts, agreed delivery plan.

Watch-outs#

  • Do not promise specific compromises (e.g. Domain Admin) or fixed exploit chains — scenarios are TI-derived later.
  • Independence/separation must hold even in the single-provider model.
  • Honestly represent capability (see the capability-matrix gaps in assets/source/).
