Phase 4 — Active Red Team Testing

Timing: minimum 12 weeks · Owner: RT / RTTM (CT approvals; TM oversight)

Realistic, phased attack execution toward agreed flags — not 12 weeks of scanning.

Activities (illustrative arc)#

PeriodRT workRTTM control
Wk 1–2Recon, infra prep, validationSafety checks, readiness
Wk 3–4Initial access simulationsRisk approvals, monitoring, evidence
Wk 5–7Discovery, lateral movement, priv-escProgress vs flags, escalation
Wk 8–10Access CIF-supporting systems, objective validationProduction impact, evidence
Wk 11–12Alternative paths, consolidation, cleanup readinessCoverage review, cleanup

Operational cycle#

Propose → RTTM checks vs RTTP/risk → CT/TM approval if required → execute + capture evidence → update timeline/flags/risk register → weekly review.

Continuous controls#

Safety/production impact · flags achieved vs not · successful/unsuccessful TTPs · approved deviations · all leg-ups (approved + documented) · detection/response observations · evidence preservation + confidentiality · resources/schedule/dependencies.

Gate / exit criteria#

  • Agreed scenarios executed (≥3 prioritised end-to-end), evidence consolidated, cleanup ready.
  • Success assessed vs CIF objectives/flags and defender detection/response — not Domain Admin.

Inputs → Outputs#

  • In: approved RTTP + RoE.
  • Out: operational timeline, evidence set, observations feeding RTTR and Replay.
