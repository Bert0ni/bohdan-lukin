Red Team method (RT / RTTM)

How the red team turns an approved TTIR into executed, evidenced, safe testing.

1. From TTIR to RTTP#

For each selected scenario, design concrete attack paths to the agreed flags, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. Assemble the RTTP: objectives, scenarios, attack paths, TTP mapping, infrastructure/tooling/comms, execution schedule, risk management, RoE, evidence approach and contingencies/leg-ups. No active attack begins until CT + TM approve the RTTP.

2. Operational cycle (per action)#

  1. Operator proposes an action / attack path.
  2. RTTM checks it against RTTP and risk.
  3. Actions needing separate authorisation → approval via CT/TM.
  4. Operator executes the approved action and captures evidence.
  5. RTTM updates attack timeline, flag status and risk register.
  6. Weekly meeting: results, blockers, deviations, next steps.

3. Illustrative execution arc (≥12 weeks)#

PeriodRT workRTTM control
Wk 1–2Recon, infra prep, validationSafety checks, scenario readiness
Wk 3–4Initial access simulationsRisk approvals, monitoring, evidence
Wk 5–7Internal discovery, lateral movement, priv-escProgress vs flags, escalation
Wk 8–10Access to CIF-supporting systems, objective validationProduction impact, objective evidence
Wk 11–12Alternative paths, consolidation, cleanup readinessCoverage review, cleanup

Illustrative only — scenarios may run in parallel, repeat, or change after approval.

4. What the RTTM controls continuously#

  • Safety and production impact.
  • Flags achieved / not achieved.
  • Successful vs unsuccessful TTPs.
  • Approved deviations and all leg-ups.
  • Detection & response observations (feeds Replay/Purple).
  • Evidence preservation and confidentiality.
  • Resources, schedule, inter-scenario dependencies.

5. Success criteria#

Success is measured against agreed CIF objectives/flags and the defender's detection/response outcomes — not reaching Domain Admin. A blocked path validated via an approved, documented leg-up still tests the subsequent defences; an undocumented shortcut to a flag does not.

6. Reporting#

Produce the RTTR within 4 weeks of the end of active testing (TIBER-EU 2025): executed scenarios, attack paths, TTPs, flags, deviations, leg-ups, evidence, vulnerabilities, root causes and recommendations. Support Replay, the mandatory Purple Teaming exercise (≤10 wks) and the entity's remediation planning.
