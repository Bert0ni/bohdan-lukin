Engagement checklist

Original, condensed go/no-go checklist for operators and the RTTM across a TIBER-EU engagement. It complements the formal phase guides — use it as a quick operational gate, not a replacement for the gate/exit criteria there.

flowchart LR P0["Pre-engagement"] --> P1["Scoping and launch"] P1 --> P2["Threat intelligence"] P2 --> P3["RT planning"] P3 --> P4["Active testing >= 12 wks"] P4 --> P5["Reporting · replay · purple"] P5 --> P6["Remediation and closure"]

Before active testing starts#

  • Contracts/NDA/insurance signed; team separation (single-provider) documented.
  • Project charter + SSD agreed; CIFs &amp; flags validated.
  • TTIR approved; CT + TM attested; ≥3 scenarios (A/I/C).
  • RTTP + RoE approved by CT + TM.
  • Leg-up triggers/deadlines, stop conditions and deconfliction channel agreed.
  • Infrastructure stood up &amp; validated; oplog + evidence store ready.
  • Blue team confirmed unaware; need-to-know list current.

During active testing (per action / weekly)#

  • Action is within RTTP &amp; RoE and within risk appetite (else → CT/TM approval or stop).
  • Evidence captured; oplog updated (UTC); flag status &amp; risk register current.
  • Deconfliction used on any ambiguity or real-incident signal.
  • Weekly sync: progress vs flags, blockers, deviations, leg-ups, next steps.
  • Noise/sophistication matched to the emulated threat profile.

Closure#

  • All cleanup/artefact removal confirmed.
  • RTTR delivered ≤4 weeks; findings written with root cause + priority.
  • Support BTTR, replay and the mandatory purple teaming (≤10 wks).
  • Input to TSR &amp; remediation plan; 360° feedback given.

This is an operator aide-mémoire. The binding sequence, timings and gates live in the phase guides and the framework timeline.

Concept credit: red-team checklist practice as popularised by Joe Vest &amp; James Tubberville — see RedTeam.Guide and their book (further reading in the section index). This checklist is original and TIBER-aligned.
