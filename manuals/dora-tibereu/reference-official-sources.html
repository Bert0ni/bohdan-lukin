Template design notes#

  • Use ECB TTIR Guidance 2025 as the normative basis and TIBER-NO as the practical structural example when building/revising the TTIR template.
  • Produce a small document set, not one Word file: TTIR + Threat Actor & Scenario Matrix + Evidence Register, so the TI team has a structured analytical base and the RTTM receives clean inputs for the RTTP without mixing the two teams' responsibilities.
