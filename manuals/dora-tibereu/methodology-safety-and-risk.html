Safety & risk management

Testing runs on production. Safety is non-negotiable and the financial entity holds overall responsibility. This file defines the controls we apply as TIP/RT.

Non-negotiables#

  • No scope changes by us. The RTTM advises feasibility; CT/CTL own the SSD, authority validates.
  • No risky action without approval. Anything outside pre-approved RoE needs CT/TM sign-off.
  • Stop conditions are absolute. When a stop condition triggers, halt and escalate immediately.
  • Deconfliction always available. CT/TM can confirm in real time whether activity is the test.

Stop conditions (examples — tailor per engagement in RoE)#

  • Risk of real service disruption to a CIF or its customers.
  • Unexpected impact on systems/data outside agreed scope.
  • Discovery of a pre-existing real compromise or live incident.
  • Safety-of-life, legal, or data-protection concern.
  • Loss of control over tooling, access or evidence.
  • Instruction to stop from CT, CTL, TM or authority.

On trigger: cease activity → preserve state/evidence → notify CT/TM via the agreed channel → await instruction. Record in the risk register and timeline.

Deconfliction procedure#

Maintain an agreed out-of-band channel and named contacts. When BT/SOC escalates suspicious activity, CT queries the RTTM who confirms (within an agreed window) whether it is the test — so a real incident is never masked, and the test is never mistaken for a breach.

Leg-ups#

A controlled CT-provided assist (access/information) to validate a later scenario stage when blocked. Rules:

  • Must be approved by CT/TM and documented (what, why, when, who approved).
  • Must not fabricate reaching a flag — its purpose is to test the next defence.
  • Recorded in the RTTP contingencies, risk register, evidence register and RTTR.

Risk register#

Keep a living register: risk → likelihood/impact → mitigation → owner → status. Review at every weekly meeting. Significant residual risks are escalated to CT/TM before proceeding.

Evidence & confidentiality#

  • Capture timestamped, attributable evidence continuously (not reconstructed later).
  • Maintain chain of custody; store securely; restrict to need-to-know.
  • Never store real client data in this repository — only fictional examples.
  • Clean up implants/infrastructure/accounts per an agreed cleanup plan; confirm with CT.
