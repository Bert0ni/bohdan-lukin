Framework timeline & timings

Reconciled against primary sources (2026-10-25): ECB TIBER-EU Framework 2025 (PDF, the Feb-2025 DORA-aligned edition), ECB TTIR Guidance Jan 2025, and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1190. The 2025 framework was updated to align with the DORA TLPT RTS, so these timings are now framework-level and regulation-aligned. (Legacy 2018 editions are archived under _archived-2018/ and must not be used.)

Scenarios (ECB TTIR Guidance Jan 2025, §2 — "shall include")#

  • At least three end-to-end threat scenarios, for the threat profiles with the highest threat severity scores, each describing the full end-to-end attack path, covering at least: service availability, data integrity, and information confidentiality (one each, at minimum).
  • At most one of the three selected scenarios may be a non-threat-led Scenario X (forward-looking; counts toward the three, not an extra).

Phase sequence (TIBER-EU 2025: three top-level phases)#

PREPARATION  (≤ 6 months)
  ├─ pre-launch / launch · scope (SSD) · provider procurement
TESTING
  ├─ Threat Intelligence  (~4–6 wks)        → TTIR
  ├─ RT test plan         (~2–3 wks)        → RTTP
  └─ Active red team testing (≥ 12 weeks)   → flags / objectives
CLOSURE
  ├─ RTTR (≤4 wks) · BTTR (≤10 wks)
  ├─ Replay (≤10 wks) · Purple Teaming (≤10 wks, mandatory)
  └─ TSR (≤8 wks) · Remediation Plan (≤8 wks) · 360° feedback · attestation

Phases commonly overlap (e.g. the TIP may supply intelligence during active testing).
