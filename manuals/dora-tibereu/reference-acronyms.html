Acronyms — TIBER-EU / DORA TLPT

Authoritative acronym reference. Terminology source: ECB TIBER-EU Framework (2025) and DORA Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 + Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1190. Reconciled against primary PDFs in ../assets/source/ (2026-10-25).

⚠️ Terminology changed in Feb 2025. The current TIBER-EU 2025 framework (aligned to the DORA RTS) uses Control Team (CT), Control Team Lead (CTL) and Test Manager (TM). The legacy 2018 framework used White Team (WT), White Team Lead (WTL) and Team Test Manager (TTM). They map 1:1 (WT→CT, WTL→CTL, TTM→TM). Use CT/CTL/TM unless you are deliberately reading an old document. (You may still meet WT/TTM in national materials that predate the update.)

Do not confuse: RTS (Regulatory Technical Standards) vs RTT (Red Team Testers); RTTP (the plan) vs RTTR (the report); TM/TTM (authority-side manager) vs RTTM (our Red Team Test Manager).
