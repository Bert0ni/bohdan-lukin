1 · Executive summary

Meridian Bank Europe operates CIFs of clear systemic relevance to the Slovak and wider EU payments ecosystem. Intelligence gathered over six weeks indicates the entity is a credible target for (i) financially-motivated organised crime pursuing payment fraud, (ii) a state-nexus actor pursuing disruptive pre-positioning against payment availability in the current geopolitical climate, and (iii) a data-theft/extortion crew targeting cardholder data.

3 · Business overview from an intelligence perspective

Meridian Bank Europe is a mid-to-large universal bank (fictional) with ~1.8m retail customers and a growing instant-payments business. It is designated systemically relevant at national level; a sustained outage of its SEPA Instant rail or card authorisation would have cross-border knock-on effects for merchants and correspondent banks. Core banking runs on a vendor SaaS (Helvetia); card authorisation is partly delegated to PayBridge; infrastructure is hybrid (on-prem DCs + Aurora Cloud).

  • Why it is an interesting target: concentration of real-time payment flows; cardholder-data environment; public profile during an EU instant-payments mandate rollout.
  • Interdependencies: heavy reliance on four ICT third parties (above); SOC/helpdesk outsourced to NordDesk (a potential supply-chain route).
  • Geopolitical exposure: headquartered in a front-line EU/NATO state; elevated state-nexus interest in regional financial-sector disruption.
  • Client base: predominantly domestic retail + SME; a treasury desk handling correspondent flows.

11 · Intelligence gaps & assumptions

  • Dark-web access advert (F-7) could not be confirmed as Meridian — low confidence; treated as indicative only.
  • Internal network topology inferred from leaked CI config (F-3) + edge fingerprinting; not validated internally (TI phase is passive).
  • One gated active-recon request was approved by CT + TM and executed by the RTT to confirm the edge-gateway version (F-2); no other active steps were taken, and the Blue Team was not alerted.
  • Assumes the CDE boundary and payment-ops segmentation are as documented in the SSD; to be validated during active testing.

12 · Scope & flag re-validation

On the basis of the selected scenarios the TIP proposes the following to the CT/TM (ECB §1.3 re-validation):

  • FLAG-1: confirm the "canary payment instruction" mechanism and the exact acceptance criterion (what proves integrity impact without moving real funds).
  • FLAG-2: confirm the defined test-token record set and that production cardholder data is out of reach by design.
  • FLAG-3: confirm "demonstrated position" (capability shown, not executed) as the availability acceptance criterion — no live disruption.
  • Consider adding an authenticity sub-objective to FLAG-1 (payment-instruction origin).

B. Actor scoring method

Each candidate actor scored on Capability (resources, tooling, sector tradecraft) and Intent (motivation, targeting rationale, recent activity) on a 4-point scale (Low / Medium / High / Very high). Threat = the combined profile; the three highest-severity profiles were taken into scenarios. Source reliability + information credibility recorded per item (Admiralty A–F / 1–6); analytic judgements carry an explicit confidence level. Full scoring lives in the scenario matrix and evidence register.
