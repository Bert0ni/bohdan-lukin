Methodology — overview

How we deliver a TIBER-EU engagement. This is our operating model, grounded in the normative framework (../reference/) but written as practice.

Three levels of the process#

mindmap root((TIBER-EU engagement)) Regulatory governance TLPT authority and TM TCT Entity owns the test Threat Intelligence TIP Digital footprint Threat actors and scenarios Red Team Operations RT and RTTM Attack paths to flags Evidence and reporting

Always keep these distinct — mixing them causes governance failures:

  1. Regulatory governance — who initiates, controls and formally recognises the TLPT (Authority/TM, TCT). Owner of the engagement is the financial entity, not us.
  2. Threat Intelligence — who researches threats and forms threat scenarios (TIP).
  3. Red Team Operations — who turns scenarios into attack plans, executes and evidences (RT/RTTM).

A TIBER test is not a large penetration test. It is a controlled simulation of attacks on critical/important functions that assesses compromise and the defender's ability to detect and respond.

Our delivery model: single provider, separated teams#

The client has agreed one provider supplies both TIP and RT, with internal separation of the two teams. To make that separation auditable we maintain a TI-to-RT Handover Register recording each artifact, who prepared it, who reviewed it, and its approval status. This is a traceability tool, not a substitute for the formal TIBER approval procedure.

Guiding principles#

  • Intelligence-led, not tool-led. Scenarios come from real, relevant threat actors — never invented exploit chains promised up front.
  • Scope and risk belong to the client. The RTTM advises on technical feasibility; the CT/CTL own the SSD and the authority validates it.
  • Safety first. Testing runs on production; every risky action has approvals, stop conditions and deconfliction. See safety-and-risk.md.
  • Evidence by default. Timestamped, attributable, chain-of-custody evidence for every flag and significant TTP — captured continuously, not reconstructed afterwards.
  • Success is measured against objectives, not Domain Admin. Detection and response outcomes are part of the result.
  • Everything maps to MITRE ATT&CK for a shared language across TTIR, RTTP and RTTR.
