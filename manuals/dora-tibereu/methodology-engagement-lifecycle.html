Engagement lifecycle (end-to-end narrative)

The full arc from regulatory initiation to attestation, from our (TIP + RT) vantage point. Per-phase detail lives under Phases; timings in Framework timeline.

  1. Regulatory initiation — Entity is notified (or opts) to run a TLPT; Authority appoints TM; entity forms CT/CTL and initiation documents. BT is not informed.
  2. RFP / procurement — Entity selects TIP and RT (one or split procurement). We respond with technical + commercial proposals; independence and qualifications are assessed; contracts signed before testing starts.
  3. Scoping & launch — CT/CTL author the SSD (CIFs, supporting assets, third parties, flags, limits), validated by the authority. Launch meeting aligns scope, comms, RoE basics, escalation and schedule.
  4. Threat intelligence (~4–6 wks) — TIP gathers target intelligence (attack surface, exposure, people, tech, third parties) and threat intelligence (relevant actors, intent, capability, TTPs, campaigns), builds a scenario longlist, runs scenario selection (≥3 chosen, A/I/C coverage), and finalises the TTIR. TTIR is approved by TM.
  5. Red team planning (~2–3 wks) — RTTM turns the TTIR into the RTTP: attack paths, TTP mapping, infrastructure, schedule, risk controls, RoE, comms, evidence, contingencies. Active attacks start only after CT + TM approve the RTTP.
  6. Active testing (≥12 wks) — reconnaissance → initial access → discovery/lateral movement → privilege escalation → objective validation against flags. Continuous safety control, approvals for risky actions, weekly status, evidence capture, deviation and leg-up documentation.
  7. Reporting, replay & purple team — RTTR (≤4 wks after active testing), BTTR by BT (≤10 wks, before the replay), joint Replay (≤10 wks), then the mandatory Purple Teaming exercise (≤10 wks; open RT+BT collaboration, detection engineering, improved-defence demonstration).
  8. Remediation & closure — Entity produces TSR (≤8 wks) and Remediation Plan (≤8 wks), 360-degree feedback from the parties, then — once the TIBER authority approves the TSR and RP — the authority (TM) signs/issues the attestation, formally closing the test and enabling mutual recognition. Remediation and regulatory follow-up may continue.

Key handoffs#

  • TIP → RTTM: TTIR + selected scenarios become the RTTP's foundation. TIP may continue to supply intelligence during active testing. Track via the TI-to-RT Handover Register.
  • RT → BT: controlled disclosure at Replay; open collaboration at Purple Teaming.
  • Providers → Entity → Authority: RTTR/BTTR feed TSR/RP, which feed attestation.
