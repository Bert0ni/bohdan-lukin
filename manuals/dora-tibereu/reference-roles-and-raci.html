Roles & RACI

Who owns what in a TIBER-EU 2025 / DORA TLPT engagement. Critical distinction: TM (authority side) ≠ RTTM (us). We never own scope or risk sign-off.

Naming (current 2025): Control Team (CT), Control Team Lead (CTL), Test Manager (TM). The legacy 2018 names were White Team / WTL / TTM. See acronyms.md.

The financial entity holds overall responsibility for test safety. The RTTM cannot unilaterally change scope, authorise a risky action, or substitute for the authority's TM.

In the 2025 framework the attestation is signed/issued by the TIBER authority (TM) after it approves the TSR and Remediation Plan; it provides the grounds for mutual recognition. (The legacy 2018 model, where the entity's board + providers signed, is superseded.)

Control Team responsibilities (ECB Control Team Guidance, Jan 2025, §2.1)#

Source: ../assets/source/ecb-control-team-guidance-2025.pdf. The CT owns the end-to-end conduct of the test and manages the phases safely, in close collaboration with — and direct contact with — the TM throughout. Its main responsibilities:

  • Execute the test in compliance with TIBER-EU; keep the entity's management body informed of progress and associated risks.
  • Ensure risk-management controls are in place and effective, keeping business impact within the entity's risk appetite.
  • Involve all relevant stakeholders so the CIFs are captured in the SSD for a realistic simulation.
  • Facilitate procurement of the TIP and external RTT per the Service Provider Procurement guidance; where internal RTT are used, evaluate suitability, ensure requirements are met, and obtain TM approval.
  • Liaise closely with TIP/RTT and the TM across the lifecycle; define communication channels and information protocols.
  • Keep execution within the agreed scope and rule on any requested deviation.
  • Be responsible for the timely delivery of all deliverables and decisions — responsibility remains with the CTL.
  • Ensure insight into and capacity to manage escalations arising from the test.

Composition (§3): a small, trusted group led by the CTL; where the entity lacks in-house capacity an external CTL may be used; members need appropriate skills/experience; the CT is bound by confidentiality / NDA. Composition is discussed with the TM.
