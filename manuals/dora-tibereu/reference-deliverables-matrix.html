Attestation — content (RTS Annex VIII, not our deliverable)#

The attestation is issued by the TIBER/TLPT authority (TM) after it approves the TSR and Remediation Plan (Art. 26(7) DORA). It must contain, at least: (a) on the performed TLPT — start/end dates, CIFs in scope, CIFs in scope not actually tested (where relevant), other financial entities involved, participating ICT third-party providers, whether internal testers were used and whether Art. 5(3) 2nd subpara was used, and the active red team testing duration in calendar days; (b) where several TLPT authorities were involved, the others and in which capacity; (c) the list of documents the authority examined for the attestation. It is the basis for mutual recognition between competent authorities.
