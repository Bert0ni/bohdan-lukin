Phase 1 — Scoping & Launch

Timing: start of Preparation · Owner: CT/CTL (authority validates); we consult

Activities#

  • CT/CTL author the Scope Specification Document (SSD): CIFs, supporting systems/services/ processes, third-party dependencies, flags, boundaries and exclusions.
  • Entity leadership approves SSD; authority validates it.
  • Launch meeting with CT, TM, TIP and RT: scope, plan, comms channels, RoE basics, responsible persons, schedule and escalation procedure.

SSD example shape#

CIFSupporting assetsFlag
Payment processingPayment gateway, IAM, processing backendControlled proof of ability to influence the payment process
Customer bankingWeb/API, authentication, backendProof of unauthorised access to a defined test resource
Treasury operationsTrading infrastructure, privileged accessProof of reaching an agreed privileged position

Gate / exit criteria#

  • SSD approved by entity and validated by authority.
  • Launch meeting held; emergency-stop and deconfliction understood by all.

Inputs → Outputs#

  • In: signed contracts, preliminary scope.
  • Out: validated SSD, launch alignment, comms/escalation plan.

Our role#

RTTM may advise on technical feasibility of flags/scope but does not own the SSD.

Reusables#

  • Project charter template (RTS Annex I — CTL-owned; frames the SSD)
  • SSD template (RTS Annex II)
