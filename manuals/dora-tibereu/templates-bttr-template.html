Blue Team Test Report (BTTR) — TEMPLATE

Basis: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1190 (DORA TLPT RTS), Annex VI — "Content of the blue team test report (Article 12(4))" (full text). Owner: the entity's Blue Team (BT) — client-produced. We do not author it; this blank is kept so the RT/CT know what the BTTR must contain, can align the RTTR's attack-step structure to it, and can quality-check it before replay/purple. Produced in the closure phase, after (and based on) the RTTR; completed ahead of the replay to maximise learning.

Annex VI compliance spine (the BTTR shall contain, at least)#

  • 1 — Per attack step described by the testers in the RTTR:
    • (a) list of detected attack actions;
    • (b) log entries corresponding to these detections.
  • 2 — Assessment of the testers' findings and recommendations.
  • 3 — Evidence of the attack (as collected by the BT).
  • 4 — BT root-cause analysis of the testers' successful attacks.
  • 5 — Lessons learned and identified potential for improvement.
  • 6 — List of topics to be addressed in purple teaming.

The BTTR is a per-attack-step mirror of the RTTR: it maps what the defence saw (or missed) against each RTT action. Keep the RTTR attack-step/TTP numbering stable so the BT can reference it 1:1. Item 6 directly seeds the closure-phase purple teaming.

Drafting layout (BT fills in)#

1. Document control#

Version · classification · author (BT lead) · reviewers · distribution · change history.

2. Detection & response per attack step (Annex VI §1)#

RTTR stepAttack action (TTP / ATT&CK)Detected?Detection source / controlLog entry referenceResponse takenTime-to-detect / respond
Yes / No / Partial

3. Assessment of RT findings & recommendations (§2)#

BT view on each RTTR finding: agree / partially agree / dispute, with reasoning and any additional context.

4. Attack evidence collected by the BT (§3)#

Telemetry, alerts, tickets, PCAPs, EDR traces the BT captured — referenced, not pasted raw.

5. Root-cause analysis of successful attacks (§4)#

BT's view of why detections/responses failed (coverage gaps, tuning, process, staffing).

6. Lessons learned & improvement potential (§5)#

Prioritised people / process / technology improvements.

7. Topics for purple teaming (§6)#

Specific items to replay or explore collaboratively in the closure-phase purple exercise.
