Test Summary Report (TSR) — TEMPLATE

Basis: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1190 (DORA TLPT RTS), Annex VII — "Details of the report summarizing the relevant findings of the TLPT (Article 26(6) of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554)" (../assets/source/Delegated-Regulation-EU-2025-1190-DORA-TLPT-RTS.md). Owner: the financial entity / CT — client-produced. We (TIP + RT) provide substantial input (most content is drawn from the TTIR, RTTP, RTTR and evidence register). Delivered to the TM within 8 weeks of the end of active testing; the TM's approval of the TSR + Remediation Plan is the precondition for the attestation (Annex VIII). Copy per engagement; do not edit this blank.

Annex VII compliance spine (the TSR shall contain, at least)#

  • (a) Parties involved — CT/CTL, TM/TLPT authority, TIP, RT, BT; third parties where relevant.
  • (b) The project plan — per the project charter.
  • (c) Validated scope — incl. the rationale for inclusion/exclusion of CIFs and the ICT systems/processes/technologies supporting them (from the SSD).
  • (d) Selected scenarios and any significant deviation from the TTIR.
  • (e) Executed attack paths and the TTPs used.
  • (f) Captured and non-captured flags.
  • (g) Deviations from the RTTP, if any.
  • (h) Blue team detections, if any.
  • (i) Purple teaming in the testing phase — where conducted, and the related conditions (LPT).
  • (j) Leg-ups used, if any.
  • (k) Risk management measures taken.
  • (l) Identified vulnerabilities and other findings, including their criticality.
  • (m) Root-cause analysis of successful attacks.
  • (n) High-level remediation plan — linking each vulnerability/finding to its root cause and remediation priority.
  • (o) Lessons derived from feedback received (360° feedback).

The TSR is the consolidated, authority-facing summary of the whole TLPT. It reuses, rather than repeats, the detailed artifacts: pull findings from the finding cards / RTTR, detections from the BTTR, and scope rationale from the SSD. Keep it summary-level; the evidence lives in the underlying reports.

Drafting layout#

  1. Document control & confidentiality — version, classification, authors, approvals, distribution.
  2. Parties & governance (a).
  3. Project plan & timeline (b) — key dates, active-testing duration (calendar days).
  4. Validated scope & rationale (c).
  5. Scenarios & TTIR deviations (d).
  6. Execution summary (e, f, g, j) — attack paths, TTPs, flags captured/not, RTTP deviations, leg-ups.
  7. Defensive outcomes (h, i) — BT detections; purple teaming in the testing phase + conditions.
  8. Risk management measures (k).
  9. Findings & root causes (l, m) — vulnerabilities/findings with criticality; root-cause analysis.
  10. High-level remediation plan (n) — findings ↔ root causes ↔ priority (feeds the full Remediation Plan).
  11. Lessons learned (o).

Companion deliverable: the Remediation Plan (RP) (Art. 13) — concrete actions, owners, timelines and non-completion risks — also to the TM within 8 weeks. The attestation (Annex VIII) follows the TM's approval of TSR + RP.
