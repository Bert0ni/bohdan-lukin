Phase 5 — Reporting, Replay & Purple Teaming

Timing (TIBER-EU 2025): RTTR ≤ 4 weeks · BTTR ≤ 10 weeks · Replay ≤ 10 weeks · Purple Teaming ≤ 10 weeks — all from the end of active testing. Owner: RT + BT + CT

Controlled disclosure to the Blue Team and learning activities.

Activities#

  • RTTR (RT/RTTM): executed scenarios, attack paths, TTPs, flags, deviations, leg-ups, evidence, vulnerabilities, root causes, recommendations. Delivered to the CT within 4 weeks of the end of active testing.
  • BTTR (BT): telemetry, alerts, incident tickets, response actions vs RT timeline. Within 10 weeks; completed ahead of the replay to maximise learning.
  • Replay: RT + BT reconstruct the attack timeline — what the attacker did, what the SOC saw, what was missed, which alerts fired without response, what could have stopped it. ≤10 weeks.
  • Purple Teaming (mandatory): open RT+BT collaboration — what other steps the RT could have taken and how the BT can improve detection/response. ≤10 weeks. Basis: ECB TIBER-EU Purple Teaming Guidance, Jan 2025 (PDF).

The guidance defines two distinct variants — always be explicit about which one is meant:

  • **Limited Purple Teaming (LPT) — in the testing phase (Phase 4, not here):** used only under exceptional circumstances to avoid impact on data, damage to assets, or disruption to CIFs / the entity / its counterparts / the sector; only after the CTL has suspended the test, when continuation is not otherwise possible, and after prior TM approval. LPT counts toward the 12-week minimum active-testing duration.
  • **Purple Teaming exercise — in the closure phase (here): a standard, mandatory element, no later than 10 weeks** after active testing ends. Topics are jointly chosen by key stakeholders and may include: vulnerabilities found during the test · issues that could not be tested during active testing · other steps the RTT could have taken and potential BT responses · alternative scenarios and their consequences · proofs of concept · anticipated remediation measures discussed with the RTT · business-continuity exercises.

Gate / exit criteria#

  • RTTR delivered ≤4 wks; BTTR ≤10 wks (before replay); Replay and Purple Teaming completed ≤10 wks.
  • Detection/response gaps documented with recommendations.

Inputs → Outputs#

  • In: operational timeline + evidence from Phase 4; BT telemetry.
  • Out: RTTR, BTTR, replay & purple outcomes — inputs to TSR and Remediation Plan.
