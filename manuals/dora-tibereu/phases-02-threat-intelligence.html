Phase 2 — Threat Intelligence

Timing: ~4–6 weeks · Owner: TIP (RT consults; CT/TM gate)

See the full method in ../../methodology/threat-intelligence-method.md.

Activities (TIP operational sub-phases)#

  • Direction: business-overview session + questionnaire (ECB Annex 1) at the start; set intelligence requirements (PIRs) from the SSD; agree OPSEC/deconfliction (no BT alerting).
  • Collection: target intelligence (digital footprint per CIF — attack surface, exposure, people, tech, third parties) and threat intelligence (GTL, relevant actors, intent, capability, TTPs, campaigns). Passive by default; active recon only by the RTT, at TIP request, after CT+TM approval.
  • Processing & analysis: contextualise CIFs & flags; actor longlist → shortlist via capability × intent (× relevance) scoring; determine modus operandi mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.
  • Scenario development: longlist (≥6) → shortlist (≥3, A/I/C coverage, ≤1 Scenario-X), targeting every CIF; profile actors (motivation/goals/sophistication/agility/perseverance/purpose).
  • Scenario selection meeting: CT, TM, TIP, RT — RTTM assesses feasibility, time, TTP match, flag reachability without unacceptable impact, leg-ups and dependencies; CTL selects the shortlist.
  • TTIR finalisation, TTIR meeting, flag re-validation; CT + TM agree/attest before the test proceeds.
  • Handover to RT; after the test, share the TTIR with the entity's CTI team.

See the full operational method (passive vs active, source grading, actor profiling, scenario anatomy, report structure) in ../../methodology/threat-intelligence-method.md.

Gate / exit criteria#

  • TTIR approved by TM.
  • ≥3 end-to-end scenarios selected, covering Availability, Integrity, Confidentiality.
  • Each scenario mapped to a CIF and agreed flag, with evidence references.

Inputs → Outputs#

  • In: validated SSD, launch alignment.
  • Out: approved TTIR, scenario shortlist, actor profiles, evidence register, handover to RT.
