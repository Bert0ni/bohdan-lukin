Rules of Engagement (RoE) — TEMPLATE

Owner: RT / RTTM, agreed with CT/TM. Attached to / referenced by the RTTP. Copy per engagement.

1. Parties & contacts#

RTTM · operators · CTL · CT · TM. Named contacts + out-of-band deconfliction channel.

2. Testing windows#

Permitted days/hours, blackout/change-freeze periods, time zone.

3. Permitted actions#

Explicitly allowed techniques, targets, and scope boundaries.

4. Prohibited actions#

Explicitly disallowed techniques (e.g. destructive actions, DoS, real data exfiltration, actions affecting out-of-scope or third-party systems, safety-of-life risks).

5. Approval requirements#

Which actions need separate CT/TM approval before execution; how approval is requested/recorded.

6. Stop conditions & emergency stop#

List of stop conditions; emergency-stop procedure: cease → preserve → notify CT/TM → await. (See ../methodology/safety-and-risk.md.)

7. Deconfliction procedure#

How CT confirms whether activity is the test; response window; escalation.

8. Data handling & confidentiality#

How evidence/data is captured, stored, transmitted, retained and destroyed; need-to-know.

9. Leg-ups#

When/how leg-ups may be requested; approval + documentation requirements; constraints.

10. Communications & status cadence#

Weekly status format, attendees, channels; incident/urgent escalation.

11. Cleanup#

Removal of implants/infrastructure/accounts; confirmation to CT.

12. Sign-off#

RTTM · CT · TM · date/version.
