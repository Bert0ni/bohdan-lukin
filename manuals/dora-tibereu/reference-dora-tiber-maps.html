DORA & TIBER-EU — maps & tables

Visual quick-reference for the regulation and the framework: mindmaps and tables that summarise how DORA (Reg. (EU) 2022/2554), its TLPT RTS (Reg. (EU) 2025/1190) and TIBER-EU fit together. Diagrams are Mermaid (render when the page is hosted/online).

TLPT in DORA — the key articles#

ArticleSubject
Art. 24General principles for digital operational resilience testing
Art. 25Testing of ICT tools and systems
Art. 26Advanced testing based on TLPT — ≥ every 3 years; covers CIFs on live production; attestation &amp; mutual recognition
Art. 27Requirements for TLPT testers (external &amp; internal)

TIBER-EU is the ECB framework used to satisfy the DORA TLPT obligation. See the full texts: DORA · TLPT RTS.
