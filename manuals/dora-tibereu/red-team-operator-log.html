Operator log (oplog)

Original guide + schema for the operator log — the contemporaneous, timestamped record of every action the red team takes. The oplog is the backbone of evidence: it feeds the evidence register, the RTTR storyline, and the Replay/Purple reconstruction.

Why it matters#

  • Reconstruction — Replay and the BTTR compare red actions to blue detections per step; without a clean oplog there is nothing to align against.
  • Deconfliction — answers "was that us?" instantly during the test.
  • Safety &amp; accountability — proves what was and was not done, and that actions stayed within the RoE.
  • Attribution of detections — lets the blue team learn which exact action they did/didn't see.

Log every action as it happens#

flowchart LR ACT["Action"] --> REC["Record: time, host, TTP, cmd, result"] REC --> EV["Attach evidence ref"] EV --> TL["Update attack timeline and flag status"]

Discipline#

  • Contemporaneous — write it as you act, not after; memory is not evidence.
  • One UTC clock — so red and blue timelines align exactly.
  • Sanitise, don't omit — record enough to reconstruct; keep real secrets/customer data out (reference the artefact in the evidence store instead).
  • Track cleanup — flag every artefact that must be removed; confirm removal at closure.

Keep the live oplog as a spreadsheet/structured file in the engagement workspace, not in this repository. The evidence register template defines the companion evidence columns.
