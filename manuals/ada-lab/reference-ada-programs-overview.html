ADA Programs — Overview & Mandate Map (internal reference)

What every App Defense Alliance program is, who stands behind it, and which ones a testing lab can actually deliver. Research date 2026-10-27; items tagged [VERIFY]. Per-program detail pages are linked in the last column.

The ecosystem in one paragraph#

The App Defense Alliance (ADA) is hosted by the Linux Foundation (Joint Development Foundation) and is backed by Google, Meta, and Microsoft. It publishes standardized app-security assessment programs (CASA, MASA, Cloud Config, DASA) delivered by Authorized Labs, runs a public Certified Products Portal, and hosts an industry Malware Mitigation collaboration. AI and desktop application assessments are under development. The concrete commercial mandate today runs through Google (CASA for OAuth restricted scopes; MASA for Google Play); Meta and Microsoft back the Alliance and bring adjacent programs.

Lab-deliverable vs. not. A testing lab earns revenue from CASA, MASA, DASA, Cloud Config (and future AI Agents / Desktop). Malware Mitigation and the Certified Products Portal are not lab services — they're an industry collaboration and a registry; include them for completeness, not as revenue lines.

Cross-ecosystem adjacencies (different schemes — NOT ADA authorized-lab revenue)#

ADA is backed by Google, Meta and Microsoft, but each also runs its own app-trust program on a different scheme. These broaden the narrative TAM and create cross-sell, but do not run on the ADA authorized-lab license — treat separately and [VERIFY] lab eligibility.
