AI Agents & Tools (emerging profile)

[VERIFY] — status caveat. As of 2026-10 the ADA Authorized-Labs page formally lists CASA, DASA, MASA, and Cloud Config. "AI Agents & Tools" is an emerging assessment area, not confirmed as a distinct authorized-lab profile. Treat it as a strategic adjacency / future profile, and confirm its existence, scheme and proficiency requirements with TrustCB before planning delivery or counting its revenue as authorized-lab revenue.

Why it matters anyway#

The wave of AI agents, LLM tools and copilots that request sensitive OAuth scopes (mailbox, drive, calendar, documents) is the fastest-growing cohort of CASA-obligated apps. Whether assessed under CASA or a future dedicated AI profile, this cohort is a major demand driver — and it plays directly to in-house capability (LLM/agent red-teaming, prompt-injection, tool/agent abuse, insecure output handling, RAG poisoning).

What an AI-agent assessment would cover (anticipated)#

  • Standard CASA/ASVS application controls for the hosting app/API, plus AI-specific risks: prompt injection, tool/function-call abuse, excessive agency, insecure output handling, data exfiltration via the agent, RAG/data-source poisoning, over-broad scope/permission use.
  • Alignment with emerging standards: OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications / Agentic AI, and any ADA/Google AI assessment criteria as they publish.

Strategic positioning#

  • Differentiator: few of the ~10 incumbent labs lead on AI-agent security; an automation-+- AI-red-team-native lab can own this niche early.
  • Premium pricing: complex, high-value work (modelled ~$5,000–$10,000; ASSUMPTION).
  • Timing: position now (thought leadership, methodology), formalize when ADA/TrustCB defines the profile.

Sequencing#

Treat as profile #5 / future — build the methodology and market presence while CASA/MASA generate revenue; convert to an authorized profile the moment the scheme supports it. In the financial model it sits in the optimistic scenario and the Year 3+ upside, not the conservative base. See ../business-case/financial-model.md.
