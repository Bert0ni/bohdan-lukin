Competitor & Market Pricing (researched)

Research date: 2026-10-27. Confirmed data = published by the lab, Google, or a named buyer. Indicative = third-party blog/aggregator. There is no official Google/ADA price list — all labs quote privately, so treat these as a pricing map, not a tariff. Get written quotes before modelling final numbers. Sources at the bottom.

Takeaways:

  • AL1 is a wide, competitive band (~$500 → $3,000+) — commoditizing at the low end (TAC), premium at full-service firms.
  • AL2 is the value tier (~$4,500 → $8,000) and is where pentest-grade firms differentiate.
  • Everything recurs annually (revalidation) — the renewal is the real prize.

The Google MASA FAQ price band is the single most authoritative price anchor we have: AL1 ≈ $500, AL2 ≈ $3–6k. Note the ~$500 TrustCB certification fee embedded in AL2 — a real per-assessment scheme cost to model in COGS.

DASA / Cloud Config / AI Agents & Tools#

No reliable public price points. Modelling assumptions (flag as ASSUMPTION):

  • DASA (lighter-touch): ~$600 – $1,200.
  • Cloud Config (usually a CASA-AL2 add-on): ~$2,000 – $4,000 incremental.
  • AI Agents & Tools (emerging, premium, complex): ~$5,000 – $10,000. [VERIFY] whether this is yet a formal ADA authorized-lab profile — see ../ada-authorization/ai-agents-profile.md.
