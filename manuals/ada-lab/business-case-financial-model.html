Recommended raise: ~$1.8–2.0M, sized to survive the conservative trough while targeting the realistic/optimistic ramp. Note the counter-intuitive but real point: a slower ramp burns more cash (longer losses) — so size the raise to the downside, not the base.

8. Sensitivities (what moves the model most)#

  1. Retention (±5 pts) — directly compounds the annuity; the single biggest long-run lever.
  2. Gross margin / automation — assessor-days per job sets both margin and capacity ceiling.
  3. Go-live date — every month of ISO/IEC 17025 slip pushes the whole revenue curve right.
  4. ACV mix (AL1 vs AL2 vs AI Agents) — shifting toward AL2/AI premium work lifts revenue without new logos.
  5. Overhead discipline — fixed cost is what keeps the conservative case alive; keep it lean until renewals carry it.

9. What to replace before investor use#

  • Real quotes (TrustCB, accreditation body, consultant, tooling).
  • Bottom-up staffing/capacity plan → derive volumes and gross margin, don't assume them.
  • 3+ competitor price points per profile → firm up ACV.
  • Comparable exit multiples for scheme-licensed assurance businesses.
  • Sourced obligated-app counts (see market-sizing.md) → firm up TAM.
