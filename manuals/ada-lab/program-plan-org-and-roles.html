Independence & impartiality (the rule that shapes the org)#

ISO/IEC 17025 requires demonstrable impartiality. Concretely:

  • The lab may not validate an application it built, remediated or consulted on.
  • Separate "readiness/advisory" and "certifying" functions with a documented firewall (different teams; ideally different brand/entity), or refer remediation to partners (see ../strategy/partnership-strategy.md).
  • Maintain a conflict-of-interest register and per-engagement independence checks.
  • Assessors sign independence/confidentiality attestations per engagement.

Competence management#

  • Each assessor has a competency record: training, mentored assessments, authorization to perform specific methods/profiles.
  • New profiles require re-authorizing assessors for that method.
  • Competence is auditable — the accreditation body will check it.

Build vs. hire#

  • Hire senior for Quality Manager (ISO/IEC 17025 experience) and Technical Lead — these de-risk the two failure modes (accreditation slip, trial-eval fail).
  • Grow assessors from existing pentest staff (capability adjacency) + competency program.
