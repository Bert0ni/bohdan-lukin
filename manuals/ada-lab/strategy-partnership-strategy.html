Partnership & Channel Strategy

The authorized-lab license is most valuable when it sits at the center of a channel rather than selling only direct. Partnerships also carry the investor-relevant narrative: a defensible position with multiple distribution and exit paths.

1. ADA / TrustCB (the enabling relationship)#

  • TrustCB is the gatekeeper and ongoing certification body — treat it as a managed relationship (surveillance, scheme updates, new profiles). Stay close to catch methodology/fee changes early.
  • ADA / Google ecosystem — being listed as an authorized lab is itself a demand channel (developers find labs via the ADA/Google docs). Optimize that listing and any co-marketing.

2. Consultancy / remediation partners (impartiality-driven channel)#

Because the lab cannot both fix and certify the same app, there is a natural two-sided flow:

  • Security consultancies & dev agencies refer clients to us for the independent validation they cannot self-provide.
  • We refer remediation back to them (or to a firewalled sister team).
  • Formalize as a referral/partner program with revenue share.

This turns a compliance constraint into a lead engine.

3. Platform & aggregator channels#

  • SaaS platforms / marketplaces whose developers face CASA (Google Workspace Marketplace ISVs, OAuth-heavy integration platforms) → bulk/volume referral deals.
  • Cloud/dev platforms and compliance-automation vendors (SOC2/ISO tooling) → integrate "get CASA-validated" into their workflow; co-sell.
  • AI-agent platforms — the fast-growing cohort needing Google-scope validation; early partnerships capture the wave.

4. Audit / assurance firm alliances#

  • Firms doing SOC 2 / ISO 27001 can bundle CASA/MASA; mapping pre-existing certs into CASA requirements is an explicit CASA feature → joint offerings.

5. The M&A / strategic angle (for investors)#

  • Authorized-lab status is scarce and acquirable. Several incumbents on the roster are the kind of acquirers (global assurance firms, security majors) that buy licenses + trained teams rather than build them.
  • A profitable, multi-profile authorized lab with a renewing client base is a clean bolt-on acquisition → credible strategic exit, in addition to the standalone annuity.

Partnership priorities (sequence)#

  1. TrustCB relationship — mandatory, from day one.
  2. Remediation-partner referral program — launch at CASA go-live (immediate lead flow).
  3. 1–2 platform/aggregator deals — once delivery is proven, for volume.
  4. Assurance-firm bundles — as the brand matures.
