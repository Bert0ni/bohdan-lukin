Break-even logic#

Operating break-even ≈ when (assessments × gross margin) covers fixed opex (salaries + surveillance + tooling). Two things pull break-even earlier:

  1. Renewals — year-2 revalidations are low-effort, high-margin and stack on new logos.
  2. Automation — cuts assessor-days/job, raising both margin and capacity.

Target: operating break-even in ~Year 2, with setup capital amortized as the moat cost.
