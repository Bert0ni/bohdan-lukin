Executive Business Case — ADA Authorized Lab

Investor-oriented summary. One page of signal; the models behind every number are linked. Figures are modelled estimates unless marked confirmed — see the honesty box at the end.

The opportunity#

Google requires third-party apps using sensitive/restricted OAuth scopes (Gmail, Drive, Workspace) to pass an annual security assessment — CASA — performed by an authorized lab; mobile apps seeking Google Play's trust badge need MASA. Self-assessment is no longer accepted for restricted scopes. Yet only ~10 labs worldwide are authorized to perform this work. That is a mandated, recurring, supply-constrained market.

The business: become an ADA Authorized Lab (CASA + MASA first; then DASA, Cloud Config, and the emerging AI Agents & Tools area) and monetize a high-retention annuity behind a scarce license — built on existing penetration-testing capability.

Why it's investable (five points)#

  1. Mandated demand — compliance, not discretionary spend. (confirmed: Google requires it)
  2. Recurring by design — annual revalidation → renewing clients, compounding base.
  3. Scarce, defensible license — the ISO/IEC 17025 + TrustCB barrier that's hard for us is the same wall that keeps the 11th–20th competitors out. (confirmed: ~10 labs)
  4. Capital-light, high-margin services — automation drives margin and capacity.
  5. Low execution risk — productizing web/mobile/cloud pentests we already do, under a QMS.

Pricing we're modelling against (researched)#

  • CASA: AL1 ~$500–$3,000; AL2 ~$4,500–$8,000 (annual). (indicative — labs quote privately)
  • MASA: AL1 ~$500, AL2 ~$3,000–6,000. (confirmed — Google MASA FAQ)
  • Full map + sources: competitor-pricing.md.

Where we win vs. the ~10 incumbents#

  • Automation-first → faster turnaround + lower cost than manual incumbents (turnaround is a top buyer complaint).
  • Pentest depth → credible AL2 where lighter labs cluster at AL1.
  • Multi-profile one-stop (web + mobile + cloud + AI) and EN/UA/CEE-EU regional reach.
  • Detail: ../strategy/competitive-landscape.md.

Use of funds#

ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation (consultant + body), TrustCB petition + trial evaluations, a small senior quality/assessor team, and the evidence-automation platform — through CASA go-live and first revenue, with runway to the conservative break-even. Breakdown: financial-model.md §3–4.

The ask#

~$1.8–2.0M to cross a regulatory-grade barrier only ~10 firms worldwide have crossed, and sit on the renewing side of a Google-mandated compliance flow — capped downside (setup cost), scarce-annuity upside, and a clean strategic-acquisition exit.
