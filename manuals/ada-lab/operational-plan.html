Master Operational Plan — Becoming an ADA Authorized Lab

Owner: Program Lead · Status: Draft v0.1 · Date: 2026-10-27 · Review cycle: monthly

1. Executive summary#

We will build and certify a new App Defense Alliance (ADA) Authorized Lab offering CASA, MASA, DASA and Cloud Config assessments. Authorization is controlled by the certification body TrustCB and is gated on ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation plus a per-profile proficiency / trial evaluation.

  • Why: ADA/CASA is a Google-mandated, annually revalidated security assessment for apps using sensitive OAuth scopes and for Google Workspace/Play distribution. Demand is recurring and growing, supply is scarce (~10 authorized labs worldwide), and the work is adjacent to an existing penetration-testing capability — low marginal cost, high retention.
  • What it takes: a quality-management system, accredited scope, trained assessors, an evidence/automation pipeline, and successful trial evaluations — ~12–18 months [VERIFY] from a zero ISO-17025 start to first authorized profile, with CASA first.
  • The ask (illustrative): see program-plan/budget-and-financials.md — a setup investment covering accreditation, headcount ramp and tooling, reaching operating break-even within ~Year 2 on a modest assessment volume.

This is positioned as an investable business direction, not just a certification project — the strategic case lives in strategy/.

2. Goal, scope, and definition of done#

Goal. Hold a valid ADA Authorized Lab authorization and deliver paid, ADA-recognized assessments at commercial scale.

In scope. All four profiles, sequenced:

  1. CASA (first — largest demand, core to the thesis)
  2. MASA (fast-follow — reuses our existing mobile-security capability)
  3. DASA and Cloud Config (add once the lab is operating)

Out of scope (for now). Running our own non-ADA product certifications; building an accreditation body; geographic expansion beyond the initial accredited scope.

Definition of done (per profile).

  • ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation covering the profile's test methods is live.
  • TrustCB trial evaluation for the profile is passed.
  • TrustCB authorization certificate is issued and the lab is listed by ADA.
  • At least one paying assessment delivered end-to-end with a valid Letter of Validation.
  • Surveillance / revalidation calendar is in place.

3. The two parallel tracks#

Authorization sits at the intersection of two workstreams that must run concurrently, not sequentially — the ISO track is the long pole, so start it on day one.

 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ TRACK A — ISO/IEC 17025 ACCREDITATION  (the gating long-pole)        │
 │  scope → QMS → method validation → internal audit → assessor audit → │
 │  accreditation granted                                               │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
          ║ (both feed the authorization)              ║
          ▼                                            ▼
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ TRACK B — ADA / TrustCB AUTHORIZATION                               │
 │  petition TrustCB → study scheme docs → build CASA delivery →       │
 │  trial evaluation (per profile) → authorization → surveillance      │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The 5 official ADA steps (from the ADA Authorized Labs page) are:

  1. Petition to become an Authorized Lab (via TrustCB's "Request to become an Authorized Lab")
  2. Begin the ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation process
  3. Complete a trial evaluation with TrustCB oversight
  4. Complete the ISO/IEC 17025 audit
  5. Ongoing maintenance of the authorization (surveillance)

Full detail: ada-authorization/overview.md and accreditation/iso17025-roadmap.md.

5. Organization & independence#

An accredited lab must demonstrate impartiality and competence. Key roles (detail in program-plan/org-and-roles.md):

  • Lab Director / Program Lead — accountable for authorization and P&L.
  • Quality Manager — owns the ISO/IEC 17025 QMS, document control, internal audits.
  • Technical Lead (CASA/MASA) — owns test methods, assessor competency, report quality.
  • Assessors — perform evaluations; competency records required.
  • Impartiality function — manages conflicts of interest (cannot assess work we built/consulted on).

Independence rule that shapes sales: a lab generally cannot both remediate/consult on and then certify the same application. This must be built into the engagement model from the start — see strategy/revenue-model.md.

7. Top risks (pointer)#

The full register is program-plan/risk-register.md. The three that most threaten the timeline/thesis:

  1. ISO/IEC 17025 slips — it is the long pole; mitigate with an experienced consultant and an early scope decision.
  2. Trial evaluation fails — mitigate with dry-runs against ADA's published methodology and hiring/coaching proven assessors.
  3. Scheme changes (ASVS version bumps, tier→AL renaming, fee/process changes by ADA/TrustCB) — mitigate by tracking official sources and keeping methodology modular.
