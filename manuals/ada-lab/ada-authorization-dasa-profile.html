DASA — Developer-Attested Security Assessment

What it is#

DASA is an ADA profile oriented around developer attestation of security posture, with lab involvement in validating the attestation rather than a full independent test. It is a lighter-touch, higher-volume profile relative to CASA AL2.

[VERIFY] DASA is the least publicly documented of the four profiles. Confirm its exact definition, the lab's role, the evidence/attestation model, and its proficiency-evaluation requirements directly from the TrustCB scheme documents before planning delivery.

Why we add it later (profile #3)#

  • Lower complexity per engagement → attractive volume/throughput play once the lab machinery exists.
  • Reuses the CASA requirement base and evidence pipeline.
  • Good entry-level / upsell funnel: developers who start with an attested assessment can be moved up to CASA AL1/AL2 as their risk level rises.

Delivery model (to be defined from scheme docs)#

  • Intake of developer-provided evidence/attestation.
  • Lab review against the applicable requirement set.
  • Validation output per the ADA scheme.
