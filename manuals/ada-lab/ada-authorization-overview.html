ADA Authorization — the scheme, profiles and official steps

What the App Defense Alliance is#

The App Defense Alliance (ADA) is an industry body (founded by Google with partners; now hosted under the Linux Foundation / Joint Development Foundation since 2024) that runs standardized app-security assessment programs. Its assessments gate real commercial outcomes — most importantly Google's CASA requirement for apps using sensitive/restricted OAuth scopes and listings/badges on Google surfaces.

Crucially for us: ADA does not authorize labs directly. It delegates lab authorization to an external certification body, TrustCB, which:

  • manages the lab authorization process,
  • reviews the labs' profile (proficiency) assessments, and
  • issues the final authorization certificate.

A lab is authorized per profile — each requires its own proficiency/trial evaluation, so we sequence them (CASA first).

The two hard requirements for any lab#

  1. ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation — the general requirement for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories. This is the gating prerequisite and the longest-lead item. See ../accreditation/iso17025-roadmap.md.
  2. Proficiency evaluation per profile — a TrustCB-overseen trial evaluation proving the lab can correctly execute that profile's methodology before it is authorized to sell it.

The 5 official steps (as published by ADA)#

  1. Petition to become an Authorized Lab — submit the "Request to become an Authorized Lab", which routes to TrustCB's ADA certification intake.
  2. Begin the ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation process — start the QMS build and engage an accreditation body (runs for many months; start immediately).
  3. Complete a trial evaluation with TrustCB oversight — perform a supervised assessment for the target profile(s); TrustCB reviews the output for competence.
  4. Complete the ISO/IEC 17025 audit — the accreditation body's on-site/remote assessment of the lab's competence and QMS; on success, accreditation is granted.
  5. Ongoing maintenance of the authorization — surveillance, revalidation, keeping methods current as the scheme updates.

Scheme documents to obtain at petition#

The ADA page references these policy/scheme documents — get and study them first:

  • Security Test Lab Authorization (the authorization rules)
  • Scheme Overview
  • Evaluation Methodology (how assessments must be performed/scored)

These define the authoritative requirements; everything in this repo is a working interpretation until reconciled against them. See ../reference/official-sources.md.

Who already holds authorization (competitive context)#

Labs listed by ADA include Bishop Fox, DEKRA, Eydle, Leviathan Security, NCC Group, NetSentries, NowSecure, Prescient Security, TAC Security, ValueMentor (~10 globally). This scarcity is the core of the commercial thesis — see ../strategy/competitive-landscape.md.
